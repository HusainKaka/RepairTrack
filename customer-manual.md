# Customer manual

## Create and verify an account

Use the business ID from the repair business, your email, phone, name, and a strong unique password. Open the verification link sent by email before sign-in. Google Sign-In is available only after the provider is configured and still binds the account to the correct business.

## Track a repair

- Sign in to view your own current/history records, invoices, payments, and notifications.
- Or open Track repair and paste the private random token from the intake record.
- On Android, scan the QR code. A public tracking view shows reference, device description, received date, current status, customer messages, estimate, timeline, and billing status.
- It never shows technician internal notes, staff identifiers, profit data, security logs, or raw database keys.

Treat the tracking link like private information. If it is shared or lost, ask the business to manage the record rather than posting the link publicly.

## Invoices and receipts

Open Invoices to view your own invoice and payment status and download PDFs. A receipt may be downloaded in A4/thermal format after a recorded payment. Contact the business if a payment or status is incorrect; do not send card credentials through repair notes.

## Profile and privacy

On web, Profile → Edit profile updates your name, phone, and optional HTTPS avatar. Email changes require a separate verification workflow and are intentionally not editable here. Profile → Request account deletion immediately revokes sessions and sends the request for privacy review. Statutory financial/audit records may be retained in minimized form.
